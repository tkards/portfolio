Travis Kim

Teaching with wonder — Learning Designer & Magician

Hello! I’m Travis. Thanks for stopping by.

I studied Psychology at UC Santa Barbara and later earned a Master's in Education from Seattle University, which led me to five years of teaching high school math. During that time, I became fascinated with how people learn best—not just through explanation, but through play, curiosity, and well-designed experiences. I also love creating classroom cultures where mistakes are celebrated as essential steps in the learning process. I often blended magic into my lessons, turning abstract concepts into moments of surprise, laughter, and discovery.

Alongside teaching, I’ve spent over a decade performing magic professionally—on stages across the country and online as a Twitch streamer, where I shared interactive performances with thousands of viewers. Today, I’m exploring the intersection of performance and learning design. I’m currently seeking learning and design roles, and this portfolio highlights how those worlds come together in my work.
